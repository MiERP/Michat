# Legal RAG Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Make MiChat retrieve Vietnamese legal documents by verified document structure and article-aware chunks, not only similarity search.

**Architecture:** Parse legal headings deterministically at ingest. Store outline metadata on documents and heading metadata on chunks. Add a persisted profile chunk for document-navigation questions; route those questions before embedding search. Keep LLM summaries as UI-only material, never as legal facts.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Electron, Drizzle SQLite, FTS5, sqlite-vec, Vitest, Gemma 4 E2B, BGE-M3.
